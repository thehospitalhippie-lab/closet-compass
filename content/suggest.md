## What helps most

- The **name** of the place and the **town or county** it's in
- **What it lends** (beds, wheelchairs, walkers, shower chairs…) and whether it takes donations
- **Who can borrow**, and any cost, deposit or paperwork
- **A link or phone number** we can use to confirm the details

If you volunteer at a loan closet and want your listing changed or removed, say so in the form. We'll update it right away.

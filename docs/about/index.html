About Closet Compass

A free, independent directory of places in Tennessee to borrow or donate medical equipment.

Why this exists

Every day, someone in Tennessee comes home from the hospital needing a walker, a shower chair or a hospital bed, and insurance doesn’t cover it or can’t deliver it fast enough. Meanwhile, another family has the same equipment sitting in a garage after a recovery or a death. Loan closets connect the two, but most are small, volunteer-run and hard to find online. Closet Compass puts them in one place.

Run from Nashville, Tennessee, by a hospice nurse.

How listings are researched

Listings are researched by Yeha, an AI research assistant, from public sources: organizations’ own websites, government pages, local news and nonprofit directories. Each listing shows:

  • Where the details came from, with links.
  • When it was last checked.
  • How confident we are:
    • ✓ Checked against their own site: the details match the organization’s own web page.
    • ◐ From news coverage: a news story described the program. Call to confirm before you go.
    • ○ From a directory: a third-party list mentioned it. Call to confirm before you go.

Loan closets change often. Volunteers move on, hours shift, inventory comes and goes. Always call before you drive anywhere.

Independence

Closet Compass isn’t affiliated with any organization it lists, and no one pays to be listed. Some guide pages include affiliate links for people who need to buy an item new, and we label them. See disclosures.

Not medical advice

This site shares practical information about finding equipment. It isn’t medical advice. Ask your doctor, nurse or therapist what equipment is safe and right for you.

Something wrong or missing?

Suggest a place or fix a listing. Corrections from closet volunteers and families are the best data we have.

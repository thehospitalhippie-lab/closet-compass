Disclosures & privacy

Affiliate links

Closet Compass is free to use and free to be listed on. To cover costs, some guide pages include affiliate links for people who need to buy an item new. If you buy through one of those links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links are labeled and marked sponsored in the page code. Free options always come first, and no listed loan closet pays to appear.

Privacy

  • This site doesn’t use cookies, ads or tracking pixels.

  • It’s hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep basic server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

  • If you use the suggestion form, we receive only what you type into it. An email address is optional. We use submissions only to update the directory and never sell or share them.

Medical disclaimer

The information here is general and practical. It isn’t medical advice and doesn’t replace guidance from your doctor, nurse or therapist. Equipment that’s safe for one person may not be safe for another.

Accuracy

Each listing shows its sources and the date it was last checked. Programs change, so please call before you go, and tell us if something is out of date.

Chattanooga and the counties around it, some reaching into North Georgia.

Goodwill of the Greater Chattanooga Area — HELPs Equipment Program

Chattanooga · Hamilton County · Southeast TN

Borrow Takes donations Free

Equipment
Wheelchairs and power chairs, Walkers and other durable medical equipment
Who it serves
Anyone with a disability or injury in Goodwill's 23-county area of Southeast Tennessee and North Georgia, per AARP.
Cost
Free
Loan terms
Goodwill asks that you return items when you no longer use them.
Where
Goodwill Opportunity Campus, 6104 Preservation Drive, Chattanooga, TN 37416
Good to know
Goodwill's HELPs program passes donated equipment on for free. AARP reported that a prescription was needed for wheelchairs and power chairs. Call to confirm the program is still running, and its current rules.

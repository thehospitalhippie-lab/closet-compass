Before you call

  1. Know the exact item. Ask your nurse or therapist what size and type you need.
  2. Ask if it's in stock. Many closets run on donations, and inventory changes daily.
  3. Ask who can borrow. Some serve anyone. Others serve one county, one diagnosis or members only.
  4. Ask about cost and returns. Some ask for a small donation, a deposit or a prescription.

After a loss

If your person was on hospice, the hospice usually arranges pickup of the bed, oxygen and other equipment it supplied. Medicare rentals may also belong to the supplier. Equipment you own can help the next family.
